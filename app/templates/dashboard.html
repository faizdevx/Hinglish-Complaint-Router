Dashboard

Multilingual message router: predicts intent, urgency and escalation probability for a text message, then maps the intent to a destination queue. Two models are trained and compared: a from-scratch BiLSTM and a fine-tuned MuRIL encoder.

Data caveat: intent labels come from Banking77 (banking support queries; non-English text is machine-translated). Urgency and escalation labels come from a synthetic (LLM-generated) Hindi/Hinglish set. No labelled real complaints are used. See About.
